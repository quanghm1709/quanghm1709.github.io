2. DATA WE PROCESS

2.1. Progress and settings on your device
The game stores gameplay progress, levels, resources and in-game currencies, towers, equipment, upgrades, quests, daily check-ins, tutorials, and audio/privacy settings on your device. The current version does not require a separate game account and does not provide progress synchronization through an account on our servers.

Storing progress locally does not mean all data stays on your device: some gameplay events and technical data are sent to analytics or advertising services as described below.

2.2. Usage analytics
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and improve the experience. Events may include app launches, startup duration, starting/ending runs, progression, skill use, upgrades, earning/spending resources, shop interactions, tutorials and rewarded advertisements. Custom events include a gameplay session identifier and game configuration version information.

The SDK may also process an app-instance identifier, device/operating system information, app version, language and general geographic region, depending on service configuration. These identifiers can distinguish app installations; they do not mean the data is fully anonymous.

2.3. Advertising
The game integrates Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertisements, such as opportunities to earn additional resources or energy. The advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses, device/advertising identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to infer approximate location. This data is used for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Use of advertising identifiers and personalization depends on the platform, settings, privacy choices and applicable requirements.

2.4. In-app purchases — if offered
When a released version supports purchases, Google Play or the Apple App Store processes payments. The game may process product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase status and receipts to deliver items, restore entitlements and prevent duplicate transactions. When our validation service is used, receipts or signed transaction data, the application identifier and platform may be sent to that service over HTTPS.

We do not directly receive full payment card details through the game's purchase feature. The store's payment and privacy policies also apply. This section applies only when purchases are actually available.

2.5. Support
If you email us, we receive your email address, message content and any files you voluntarily provide to resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, payment card information or unnecessary identity documents.

Reading this policy or continuing to play does not replace separate consent where such consent is required.

5. YOUR CHOICES

Analytics: in the game settings, turn off “SHARE GAMEPLAY ANALYTICS” to request that Analytics collection stop. The current configuration enables Analytics by default until you change your choice. Turning off Analytics does not automatically delete previously submitted data or also disable advertising data processing.

Advertising: the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display privacy forms when required. “AD PRIVACY” appears when the SDK requires privacy options to be provided. You can also manage advertising identifiers and related permissions in your device settings. Non-personalized advertisements may still require data processing for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Viewing rewarded advertisements is your choice. You may skip them; the associated reward may then not be granted. When permitted, the SDK may initialize and preload advertisements before you choose to watch one.
